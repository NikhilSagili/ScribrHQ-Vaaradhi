What slips away today

Every day without your own app, Vaaradhi leaves sales on the table.

You've built the food, the trust and same-day delivery across Hyderabad. These are the four places where that demand quietly leaks away.

The forgotten order

The regular who meant to order, and didn't.

Every repeat sale depends on a customer remembering to send a WhatsApp message. Busy mornings, a long day at work, and the order never comes.

The borrowed customer

The customer who belongs to Swiggy.

Every delivery-app order takes a commission. The customer's number, history and next order stay with the platform, not with you.

The guessed morning

The breakfast made for nobody.

Without tomorrow's orders in hand, you cook and stock on a guess. Too much is waste. Too little is a customer turned away.

The booked morning

Mornings already taken by someone else.

Milk and breakfast subscription apps are already on Hyderabad phones. A morning habit goes to whoever asks first. Once it's set, it rarely moves.

Your customers already love Vaaradhi. The only thing missing is a way for them to say yes every morning.

The app

One app with your name on it. Two ways to buy. One place to run it all.

Your customers get the Vaaradhi app on their phone. You get the Vaaradhi owner app. Scribr builds and runs both.
