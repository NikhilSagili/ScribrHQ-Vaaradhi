## What "verified" means here

- **Verified:** the product name and the displayed price appeared on the EkSecond page on 28 Oct 2026.
- **Not verified:** live stock, delivery areas, delivery fees, cutoff times, taxes, and any price after 28 Oct 2026. The demo is **not synchronised** with EkSecond.
- `availability: "listed"` means "listed on the ordering page at capture time", not "in stock".
- No product descriptions were shown on EkSecond, so `description` is empty for every product.
