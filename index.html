How a day works

Customers order for right now during the day, and plan tomorrow's basket by 9 PM. Vaaradhi gets every order in one place, packs only what was ordered, and gets paid for what was delivered.

  1. 7:00 AM – 9:00 PMOrder now

    Customers order for right now and pay from their wallet. Vaaradhi accepts, packs and delivers the same day, and the customer can follow each step.

  2. Anytime till 9:00 PMPlan tomorrow

    Subscribe, add extras for tomorrow, skip a day or pause for a trip. No calls or WhatsApp messages.

  3. 9:00 PMTomorrow's list locks

    Vaaradhi gets the prep sheet: exact items and quantities, so nothing is made that won't be bought.

  4. 5:00 AMPack by area

    Stops come grouped by area, from Manikonda to Gachibowli and Kondapur, so the run is planned already.

  5. 6:30 – 8:00 AMDeliver

    Each stop is marked delivered. Customers who paused are left off the list automatically.

  6. After deliveryBilling runs itself

    Customers pay from a prepaid balance held by Vaaradhi, only for what arrived. Every payment goes straight into Vaaradhi's own payment account.

For Vaaradhi

  • Your own app with your name on it
  • One-time orders and daily subscriptions in one place, without WhatsApp back-and-forth
  • Know tomorrow's demand by 9 PM tonight, so there's less waste
  • No aggregator commission on orders, and the customer relationship stays yours
  • Payments settle straight into your own payment account. Scribr never holds your money.

For your customers

  • Order anything for right now with same-day delivery
  • Fresh breakfast, fruit and vegetables every morning without ordering each day
  • Add something extra for tomorrow in two taps
  • Pause for a trip and pay nothing while they're away
  • One wallet and a clear record of every charge
